My Favourite Cartoon Character

Mickey Mouse

Mickey mouse is my favourite cartoon character because he is funny, helpful, and always ready to support his friends.

He uses toodles from the future to solve his friends problems in creative ways.

Character Details

  • Name: Mickey
  • Best Friend: Mini
  • Favourite Food: cheese
  • Special Power: very smart

Fun Fact: mickey has a magical laboratory that stores many useful gadgets.

Mickey mouse teaches us that friendship, kindness, and problem-solving are very important.
